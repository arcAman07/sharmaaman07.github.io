In Search of a Verifier

A verifier is a reward function that is cheap, exact, and automatic. Where all three hold, frontier models make real discoveries. Where they do not, progress stalls.

Cheap
Run it a million times for almost nothing.
Exact
Actually correct, not a proxy you can game.
Automatic
No human in the loop. The machine decides.
